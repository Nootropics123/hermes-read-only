Personal Google integration

Hermes Read Only

A private-use integration that lets its owner ask an AI assistant questions about their Gmail messages, Google Drive files, and Google Docs.

About the application

Hermes Read Only connects a locally running Hermes Agent to Google APIs. It supports searching and reading email, finding and downloading Drive files, and reading Google Docs when the owner requests information.

This application is for its owner's personal use. It is not a public service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google. This website is informational only; it does not provide Google sign-in or access to the owner's account.

Permissions

The integration requests only the following Google OAuth scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/documents.readonly

These permissions do not authorize sending email or editing or deleting Google account content.

Privacy policy

Data accessed and purpose

At the owner's request, the integration can retrieve email bodies, headers, labels and attachments; Drive file contents, metadata and sharing information; and Google Docs content and comments. Retrieved information is used to answer questions, summarize material, and help the owner understand their information.

Processing and sharing

The connector runs on the owner's computer and communicates with Google APIs. Retrieved content included in the assistant's context is transmitted to the configured AI service providers to generate responses. Provider data handling and retention depend on the owner's selected provider and account settings; processing is not exclusively local.

Google data is not sold, used for advertising, or intentionally made public by this integration. Data is disclosed only as needed for the requested assistant functionality or as required by law. The public informational website does not contain Google messages, documents, OAuth credentials, or access tokens.

Storage, security and retention

OAuth credentials are stored locally in permission-restricted files. Retrieved content may be saved in local assistant conversation history, logs, downloads, or owner-requested notes. Such local copies persist until removed by the owner. Content sent to AI providers may be retained according to their policies. Revoking Google access does not automatically remove previously saved copies.

Control and deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time using Google Account connections. The owner can also remove the local connector, credentials, downloaded files and conversation records. Questions or deletion requests can be sent to the contact below.

Limited use

Hermes Read Only's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data accessed through this integration is not used by the integration to train or improve generalized AI models.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the integration's functionality or data handling changes. The owner should review the configured AI provider's terms and privacy settings before transmitting sensitive information.

Terms of use

This integration is provided for personal use by its owner, as is, without warranties of availability, accuracy, or fitness for any purpose. AI-generated answers can be inaccurate; important information should be checked against the original sources.

Use is subject to Google's applicable terms and the terms of the configured AI services. The owner is responsible for their account security, provider selection, and handling of data they access. This site does not offer access to other users.
